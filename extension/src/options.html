Meeting Capture

Live meeting transcription with scribe

If the server has an access token, copy its URL from the server's web UI (pair extension): it ends in #token=….

Allow access to ?

To transcribe, the extension streams each meeting's audio to your scribe server over a WebSocket, and when a recording stops it sends the server the finished transcript. Chrome needs your permission once for the extension to connect to this host. It connects only while recording or sending a transcript, and to nothing else on the web.

Microphone

Once access is granted, every recording includes your own voice as a separate stream, labeled as you, even while others are talking. Chrome asks for permission once, here: the recording itself runs in a background page that can't ask. The microphone is open only while recording, and while you're muted in the meeting it sends silence instead.

Other meeting sites

Google Meet and Zoom are built in. On any other meeting site listed here, one host per line, the extension also reads who is in the call and whether you're muted: in a call with one other person their words get their name, and your microphone sends silence while you're muted. Chrome asks for access to each site once.
