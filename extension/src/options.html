To transcribe, the extension streams each meeting's audio to your scribe server over a WebSocket, and when a recording stops it sends the server the finished transcript. Chrome needs your permission once for the extension to connect to this host. It connects only while recording or sending a transcript, and to nothing else on the web.

Microphone

With Capture my microphone on in the popup, your own voice is recorded as a separate stream and labeled as you, even while others are talking. Chrome asks for permission once, here: the recording itself runs in a background page that can't ask. The microphone is open only while recording, and while you're muted in the meeting it sends silence instead.
