# One transport for tab and mic audio

A saved meeting now has a single audio transport: one play/pause button, a speed control, and a scrubber showing a lane per stream, all on the meeting's clock. Checkboxes mute the meeting audio and your own mic — both on by default — and clicking a transcript line still plays from there. The transcript now runs down a rail with turns spaced by how much time passed between them (long silences collapse and are labelled), and clicking a speaker's chip dims everyone else's turns.
